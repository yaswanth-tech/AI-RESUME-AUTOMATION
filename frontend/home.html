Frequently Asked Questions

Good to know
before you begin.

Which resume files can I upload?

The Resume Analyzer accepts PDF and DOCX files up to 5 MB.

What does the Resume Analyzer provide?

It provides ATS evaluation, job description matching with matched and missing skills, reviewable rewrite suggestions, resume templates and downloadable exports or reports.

Which assessments are available?

ASCEND includes a technical test, a communication test and mock interview practice. The technical test offers easy, medium and hard levels.

Do I need an account to start?

Create a free account using Sign Up to access the application tools and save your work.

How are personal details handled for AI insights?

When AI is enabled, the resume analysis service excludes raw text, name, email and phone fields from its prompt source and redacts recognized email addresses, phone numbers, URLs and street addresses from selected text.

Can I review suggestions before applying them?

Resume summary and bullet rewrites are shown for review so you can choose whether to apply them.
